Powerful Features to Streamline Rentals

Rentify supports out-of-the-box features to manage availability, bookings, inventory, and orders from a single dashboard, making rental management effortless.

RFQ Negotiation

Submit price requests and negotiate directly with sellers for the best rental deal.

Media Library

Upload an image once to a centralized storage and get it updated across pages or platforms with a single click.

Real-Time Availability

Check real-time product availability for the requested rental dates before confirming any order.

Flexible Pricing Option

Set up hourly, daily, weekly & monthly pricing based on the product or variant that best suits your business model.

Storefront Builder

Tailor your rental platform by customizing themes, menus, banners, fonts, colors, and pages.

Sub-Sfaff Accounts

Create sub-staff and assign roles and responsibilities accordingly. You can share and limit permissions flexibly.

  • Inventory availability and management
  • 90-day booking track record
  • Real-time inventory updates to mitigate booking conflicts
