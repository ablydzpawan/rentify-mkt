Listing & Inventory
Management

Rentify makes renting out your equipment simple and hassle-free. Build your online rental store in just 5 quick steps and start accepting bookings with confidence.

Rental Listing

Businesses can flexibly list rental items on the platform. The listing includes adding descriptions, photos, videos, pricing, security deposits and other specifications of the rental items. The store admin can also add product variants and separate images by size, model, type, etc.

Rental Checklist

This feature enables the store admin to create a checklist of their rental items. They can inspect rental items before renting and cross-check them upon return to verify damages and missing accessories. It protects product owners and renters from disputes.

Tiered Pricing

The tiered pricing feature allows businesses to define a varied pricing structure and offer options on an hourly, daily, weekly and monthly basis. It enables rental businesses to maximize revenue by providing more value to customers through increased rental duration.

Custom Pricing

Custom pricing features provide flexibility to define pricing by rental items and different versions (variants) of the same product. Instead of a flat rate, they can define pricing by size, model, type, and more under a single product listing.

Interactive Calendar View

The interactive calendar view provides a centralized visual dashboard. The store admin can view the last 90 days' rental booking history, current booking status, slot availability, overdue returns, buffer periods, and more, enabling them to avoid double booking and manage inventory accordingly.

Simplify Your
Order Management

Manage bookings, track order status, handle returns, process cancellations, and keep every rental organized from a single, easy-to-use dashboard.

  • Rentify provides a robust interactive calendar so store admins can view booking history, current status, and slot availability at a glance.

  • This feature enables admins to track active, pending and fulfilled orders. They can search orders using ID, name, email and product, and apply filters available in the order status, order stage, and by date menu.

  • The store admin can process walk-in offline orders and update records on the platform. Manual order creation features help businesses maintain data and stock in a central database, preventing inventory discrepancies.

  • The rental buffer period restricts booking the rental item during this period. It provides admins the flexibility to inspect, maintain, and prepare rental items before renting them out again.

  • Rental order return processing manages the end-to-end process. It automates customer returns, manages shipping, controls inventory restocking, adjusts security deposits and ensures refunds.

Renter/(Buyer) Registration

Rentify enables a streamlined and quick onboarding process. Users can register seamlessly using existing accounts and authenticate instantly via OTP verification.

Profile Management

Customers can seamlessly manage their profiles by accessing a centralized dashboard, updating contact details, shipping address, and other necessary documents.

Advanced Search and Filtering

Advanced search & filtering options allow renters to find rental items quickly, applying filters to find the most precise results according to their requirements.

RFQ Management

RFQ (Request for Quote) features allow renters to request rental pricing and negotiate directly via the RFQ module, counteroffering the proposed rental fees.

Wishlist

A save-for-later feature that lets renters bookmark items they are interested in for future rentals or repeat events instead of booking them immediately.

Real-time Calendar Availability

An interactive calendar on the product detail page that lets customers check real-time availability of rental items and helps prevent double or false bookings.

Reviews and Ratings

Trust-building features that help businesses foster trust & transparency, allowing users to leave feedback and rate their rental experience.

Flexible Fulfilment Method

Customers can choose the rental order fulfilment method, flexibly choosing between home delivery and self-pickup, subject to options enabled by the admin.

Flexible Payment Option

Rentify allows integrations with multiple payment gateways that enable renters to pay securely using their preferred method, including cards, digital wallets, or deferred payments.

Order Confirmation Notification

This feature initiates automated emails or SMS alerts to the buyer confirming the successful booking or quotation of their rental.

Rental Tracking

Helps customers keep track of their rental items, providing the live status of their booked and returned rental items and checking return due dates.

Rental Cancellation

Customers can flexibly cancel rental bookings — a self-service feature that lets renters cancel items according to the store's specific cancellation policy.

Pricing

Checkout Workflow

The store owner can flexibly decide on the checkout workflow. They can configure and decide between standard checkout & RFQ-based checkout based on their unique business model & preferences, ensuring maximum profit and sales.

eSigning/Digital Signature

The digital or electronic signatures option allows rental store owners to securely capture customer agreements & contracts remotely. Customers simply review the rental terms on their device & sign digitally.

Document Attachment

The document attachment feature fosters trust, legal compliance, and identity verification. It enables store owners to specify documents that are essential when listing rental products.

Delivery & Pickup Management

This feature empowers store owners to define the fulfilment method. They can either toggle between doorstep delivery and in-store pickup or enable both, offering flexibility to customers.

Delivery Charges Configuration

The store owner can also configure delivery charges seamlessly. The system supports both flat charges or dynamic distance-based delivery charges. Once configured, the system automatically calculates pricing and reflects it on the rental bill.

Non-Operational Days Management

This feature enables store owners to define holidays and non-working days in the calendar. It automatically blocks bookings during the selected date or period.
