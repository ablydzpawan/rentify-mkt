Learn more about key features and functionality of Rentify

Build & Customize Your Store Homepage Appearance

Create a storefront that truly represents your brand with Rentify's easy-to-use homepage builder. Customize banners, featured products or categories, promotional sections, and page layouts to design a homepage that matches your unique style. With flexible customization options and an intuitive interface, you can build a professional, visually appealing storefront that makes it easy for customers to explore your store, discover products, and enjoy a seamless shopping experience on any device.

Create and Configure Rental Products on Rentify

Easily build and manage your rental catalogue with Rentify's user-friendly product management system. Create rental listings, set flexible pricing, define rental durations, upload high-quality product images, track inventory, and organise products into categories—all from one place. Whether you're starting a new rental marketplace or growing an existing one, Rentify simplifies the entire product setup process, making it easier to showcase your inventory and provide a smooth, hassle-free rental experience for your customers.

Create Discount Codes and Rent Products on Rentify

Increase customer engagement and boost rentals with Rentify's built-in discount management system. Easily create promotional discount codes, define eligibility rules, set usage limits, and apply discounts to rental bookings. Customers can seamlessly redeem promo codes during checkout, making it easier to attract new renters, encourage repeat bookings, and maximize conversions all while managing your rental operations from a single, user-friendly platform.

Customize Your Store Homepage with Hero Banner Images

Make a great first impression with eye-catching hero banners on your Rentify storefront. Easily upload and customise banner images to showcase your brand, highlight special offers, promote seasonal campaigns, or feature your most popular rental products. With Rentify's flexible banner management tools, you can create a visually engaging homepage that captures visitors' attention, encourages them to explore your store, and guides them towards the products and services that matter most.

Add Staff Members and Configure User Permissions

Manage your team with confidence using Rentify's role-based staff management system. Easily add staff members, create individual accounts, and assign permissions based on their responsibilities. Control who can access specific features and administrative functions, ensuring your store remains secure while keeping daily operations organised and efficient. With the right level of access for every team member, you can streamline workflows, improve collaboration, and maintain complete control over your rental business.

Create Instant Rental Orders with Ease

Create rental orders in minutes with Rentify's quick and intuitive order management system. Easily select rental products, assign customers, set rental durations, and confirm bookings in just a few simple steps. By simplifying the order creation process, Rentify helps your team save time, improve operational efficiency, and deliver a fast, hassle-free rental experience for every customer.

Manage Rental Orders Efficiently

Stay in control of your rental business by managing orders from a centralized dashboard. Rentify allows you to view order details, track order statuses, update rental information, process returns, and handle customer requests with ease. Simplify order management, improve operational efficiency, and ensure a smooth rental experience from booking to completion.

Set Up Store Plugins, Themes, and FAQs

Customize your rental marketplace with ease using Rentify's all-in-one store management tools. Configure plugins to extend your store's functionality, personalise themes to match your brand, and organise FAQs to provide customers with quick answers to common questions. From a single, easy-to-use dashboard, you can create a professional storefront, improve the customer experience, and optimise your marketplace for better engagement and performance.

Manage Request for Quote and Purchase Workflows

Simplify your rental and sales operations by efficiently managing Request for Quote (RFQ) submissions and purchase requests. Rentify enables you to review customer inquiries, generate quotations, convert approved quotes into orders, and manage purchase requests from a centralized dashboard. Streamline communication, accelerate order processing, and deliver a seamless experience for both rental and purchase transactions.
