Start Your Online Rental Store in 4 Easy Steps

With Rentify, you can start your online rental store within minutes, not months.

Choose Your Plan

Getting started with Rentify takes less than a minute. Choose a plan that best suits your rental business idea, create an account and enjoy a free trial for 6/12 months.

  • Choose any plan — no credit card or commitment required.
  • Enter your first & last name, email and create a secure password.
  • Verify your account by entering the authentication code.
  • Once verified, you're taken directly to your Rentify dashboard to begin setup.

Set Up Your Business

Once your account is ready, provide the details that define how your online rental store operates.

  • Enter your business name, country and preferred currency for transactions.
  • Select fulfilment — standard eCommerce checkout or RFQ (Request for Quote).
  • Configure tax settings: inclusive, exclusive, or a custom tax structure.
  • These settings form the operational foundation — update anytime from admin.

Personalize Store Appearance

Rentify offers complete control over your storefront. Tailor every pixel to reflect your unique identity — no code required.

  • Choose a theme & font, upload logo and banner images to set the tone.
  • Manage pages — About Us, Contact, Terms & Conditions, FAQs and more.
  • Personalize header & footer navigation to guide visitors across your store.
  • Every element of your storefront is customizable, without technical expertise.

List Products and Go Live

Once your storefront is ready, it's time to add products and launch your online rental store.

  • List rental products with descriptions, photos, pricing and availability.
  • Your inventory calendar automatically keeps every booking organized.
  • Connect a custom domain to match your brand identity.
  • Go live, accept bookings, manage walk-ins — all from one admin panel.
