Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Rentify (“Rentify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a product of FATbit Technologies, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website, platform, products, and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, create a Rentify account, use our rental management platform, contact us, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using Rentify, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on how you use Rentify, its services or any offerings, we may collect the following types of information:

When you create an account, contact us, request a demo, subscribe to a plan, or use our services, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and payment-related information
  • Business and store information
  • Login credentials
  • Domain and website information
  • Information you provide through contact forms, support requests, or other communications

1.2 Store and Business Data

If you use Rentify to create and operate an online rental store, you may provide information such as:

  • Product and inventory information
  • Product images and media
  • Rental pricing
  • Availability and booking information
  • Customer and order information
  • Store content and configuration
  • Staff and user account information
  • Other information necessary to operate your rental business

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate rights and permissions to collect, process and share information that you upload or manage through Rentify.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you access our website or platform, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring pages
  • Date and time of access
  • Usage and interaction data
  • Cookies and similar technologies

/ 02 How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Rentify services
  • Create and manage your account
  • Help you build, customize, launch, and manage your online rental store
  • Process subscriptions, payments, and transactions
  • Manage bookings, inventory, availability, and orders
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Send service-related communications
  • Improve our website, platform, products, and services
  • Monitor platform performance, security, and reliability
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, or technical issues
  • Provide product updates, offers, or marketing communications where permitted by law
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

/ 03 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Rentify may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand usage, improve performance, and support security and marketing activities.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website or platform.

/ 04 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration.

We may share information with trusted service providers and third parties when necessary to operate and provide Rentify, including providers supporting:

  • Hosting and infrastructure
  • Payment processing
  • Analytics
  • Customer support
  • Communication and email services
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Domain and related website services

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, court order, or governmental authority, or where necessary to protect our rights, users, property, or the security of our services.

/ 05 Business and Customer Data

Rentify provides tools that allow businesses to operate online rental stores. If you use Rentify to collect information from your own customers, you are responsible for providing appropriate privacy notices and obtaining any required consent or permissions.

Where applicable, you are responsible for determining the lawful basis for collecting and processing your customers' personal information.

/ 06 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

/ 07 Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
