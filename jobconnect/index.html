A Trusted Digital Marketplace for Skilled Services

Job Connectz is a two-sided digital service marketplace that connects customers, businesses and institutions with artisans, freelancers and other skilled professionals — making service discovery and delivery more accessible, reliable, transparent and secure.

The marketplace module expands Job Connectz from a service-hailing experience into a more flexible service economy platform. Customers can post service requests, propose prices, suggest new service categories and choose providers that best match their needs. Providers can create service categories, publish offers and receive bookings — while the platform supports verification, job management, payments, service completion and customer feedback.

01 · Platform

A simple, transparent flow from discovery to a job well done.
